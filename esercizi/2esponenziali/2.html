Risolvere l'equazione \[ 1 = \dfrac{2^{9x - 7} \cdot 4^{10x - 6}}{\sqrt[3]{8}} \]
Soluzione
La soluzione dell'equazione è \(x = \dfrac{20}{29}\)

\[ 1 = \dfrac{2^{9x - 7} \cdot 4^{10x - 6}}{\sqrt[3]{8}} \]
\[ 1 = \dfrac{2^{9x - 7} \cdot \color{red}{}4\color{black}{}^{10x - 6}}{\sqrt[3]{8}} \]
\[ 1 = \dfrac{2^{9x - 7} \cdot \color{red}{}(2^2)\color{black}{}^{10x - 6}}{\sqrt[3]{8}} \]
\[ 1 = \dfrac{2^{9x - 7} \cdot \color{red}{}(2^2)\color{black}{}^{10x - 6}}{\sqrt[3]{8}} \]
\[ 1 = \dfrac{2^{9x - 7} \cdot (2^{\color{red}{}2\color{black}{}})^{\color{blue}{}10x - 6\color{black}{}}}{\sqrt[3]{8}} \]
\[ 1 = \dfrac{2^{9x - 7} \cdot 2^{\color{red}{}2\color{black}{}\cdot\color{blue}{}(10x - 6)\color{black}{}}}{\sqrt[3]{8}} \]
\[ 1 = \dfrac{2^{9x - 7} \cdot 2^{20x - 12}}{\sqrt[3]{8}} \]
\[ 1 = \dfrac{2^{9x - 7} \cdot 2^{20x - 12}}{\sqrt[3]{\color{red}{}8\color{black}{}}} \]
\[ 1 = \dfrac{2^{9x - 7} \cdot 2^{20x - 12}}{\sqrt[3]{\color{red}{}2^3\color{black}{}}} \]
\[ 1 = \dfrac{2^{9x - 7} \cdot 2^{20x - 12}}{\sqrt[\color{blue}{}3]{2^{\color{red}{}3\color{black}{}}}} \]
\[ 1 = \dfrac{2^{9x - 7} \cdot 2^{20x - 12}}{2^{\frac{\color{red}{3}}{\color{blue}{}3}}} \]
\[ 1 = \dfrac{2^{9x - 7} \cdot 2^{20x - 12}}{2^{1}} \]
\[ 1 = \dfrac{2^{9x - 7} \cdot 2^{20x - 12}}{2} \]
\[ \color{red}{}1\color{black}{} = \dfrac{2^{9x - 7} \cdot 2^{20x - 12}}{2} \]
\[ \color{red}{}2^0\color{black}{} = \dfrac{2^{9x - 7} \cdot 2^{20x - 12}}{2} \]
\[ 2^0 = \dfrac{2^{\color{red}{}9x - 7} \cdot 2^{\color{blue}{}20x - 12}}{2} \]
\[ 2^0 = \dfrac{2^{\color{red}{}9x - 7 \color{black}{} + \color{blue}{}20x - 12}}{2} \]
\[ 2^0 = \dfrac{2^{\color{red}{}9x - 7 + 20x - 12}}{2^{\color{blue}{}1}} \]
\[ 2^0 = 2^{\color{red}{}9x - 7 + 20x - 12\color{black}{} - \color{blue}{}1} \]
\[ 2^{\color{red}{}0} = 2^{\color{red}{}9x - 7 + 20x - 12 - 1} \]
\[ \color{red}{}0 \color{black}{}= \color{red}{}9x - 7 + 20x - 12 - 1 \]
\[ 0 = \color{red}{}9x\color{black}{} - 7 \color{red}{}+ 20x\color{black}{} - 12 - 1 \]
\[ \color{red}{}-9x\color{black}{} \color{red}{}- 20x\color{black}{}= - 7 - 12 - 1 \]
\[ \color{red}{}- 29x\color{black}{}= - 7 - 12 - 1 \]
\[ - 29x = \color{red}{} - 7 - 12 - 1 \]
\[ - 29x = \color{red}{} -20 \]
\[ \dfrac{-29x}{\color{red}{}-29} = \dfrac{-20}{\color{red}{}-29} \]
\[ x = \dfrac{20}{29} \]
